Crypto catalog for checkout, quotes, swaps, and offramp. **LIVE** merchants see `MAINNET` + `OFFCHAIN` chains; **TEST** merchants see `TESTNET` + `OFFCHAIN`.

Public API:

- `GET /api/public/catalog/chains`
- `GET /api/public/catalog/tokens` (optional `?chain_id=`)

<Note>
  This page lists **14 chains**, **28 checkout tokens**, and **43 Fonbnk assets**. Fetch the live catalog with `GET /api/public/catalog/chains` and `GET /api/public/catalog/tokens`.
</Note>
