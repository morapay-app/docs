Crypto catalog for checkout, quotes, and swaps. **Live** merchants use mainnet chains; **Test** merchants use testnet chains. Fiat checkout methods are listed separately on [Countries & fiat](/reference/countries-and-fiat).

Public API:

- `GET /api/public/catalog/chains`
- `GET /api/public/catalog/tokens` optional `?chain_id=`
