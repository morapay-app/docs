A **payment link** is a stable URL (or slug) that opens Morapay checkout for a configured amount and currency. Create and manage links with the merchant API at **`/v2/links`**.

## Lifecycle

1. `POST /v2/links` — define amount, currency, and presentation fields.
2. Share the hosted URL with customers.
3. Customer checkout uses **`POST /v2/checkouts`** and public pay routes (see [Checkouts and payments](/guides/checkouts-and-payments)).
4. Listen for `payment_link.paid` (and related) on your [webhook](/developer-tools/webhooks) endpoint.

## Products

Attach catalog items with `product_id` when you maintain products via `GET/POST /v2/products` (OpenAPI).
