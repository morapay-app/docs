---
title: "Subscriptions"
description: "Plans and subscriber enrollments on /v2/plans and /v2/subscriptions."
---

**Plans** define what you sell (amount, interval, trial). **Subscriptions** bind a customer to a plan and drive renewals.

Merchant routes:

- `GET/POST/PATCH /v2/plans`
- `GET/POST /v2/subscriptions`
- `POST /v2/subscriptions/{id}/pause`, `/cancel`, `/invoice`

Public customer routes under `/v2/public/plans/{id}` and `/v2/public/subscriptions/{id}` power hosted flows without merchant signing.
