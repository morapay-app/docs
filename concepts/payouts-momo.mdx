Morapay **payouts** move settled merchant balance to bank accounts and **mobile money** wallets. The v2 surface uses plural resources:

| Resource | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| `/v2/payouts` | Single payout instructions |
| `/v2/schedules` | Recurring payout schedules |
| `/v2/recipients` | Saved beneficiaries and `resolve` helper |

Money-moving `POST` calls require `Idempotency-Key` and signed headers.
